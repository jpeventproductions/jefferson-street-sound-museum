# Jefferson Street Sound Museum — Concept 07

A multi-page, GitHub Pages-ready redesign inspired by historic Jefferson Street, archival print, neighborhood record shops, and the museum’s living collection.

## Preview

Open `index.html` locally or serve this folder with any static web server.

## Interaction notes

- The top navigation hides while scrolling down and returns while scrolling up.
- “Explore” opens a full-screen, keyboard-accessible navigation drawer.
- The opening screen uses the museum’s vintage poster artwork, with quick links styled as old records.
- The mobile poster now ends directly at its action row, eliminating the former blank space.
- The homepage leads with “Since 2011,” Lorenzo Washington’s official portrait, and the current Founder’s Letter.
- Authentic Jefferson Street Sound Museum long-form and tree logos are used throughout the navigation.
- Record navigation is larger and more legible on desktop and mobile.
- Donation and membership calls to action receive a dedicated “Support the Sound” section.
- A floating record follows the visitor after scrolling, opening the museum menu while keeping a Home shortcut close by.
- The homepage Enter record spins, rises and fades with scroll while the poster and archival texture move at a slower depth.
- Desktop visitors get responsive hover lift, tilt, photo depth and a warm light-and-shadow wash that follows the pointer and gently ebbs with scrolling.
- The homepage keeps its place while Museum, Legacy, Community, Visit, Events and the Founder’s Letter open in accessible vintage page panels.
- A prominent Next Event feature and readable monthly calendar keep the upcoming Saturday museum date visible on the homepage and Events page.
- The Founder’s Letter is now one of the four record links on the opening screen.
- Every record-style control has a rotating vinyl surface while its wording stays upright and readable; motion stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
- All non-title text now uses a minimum 14pt reading size for older visitors, including navigation, record labels, calendars, buttons and footer details.
- The “Nashville’s history has a backbeat” feature now places Lorenzo Washington’s image beside the headline within the same desktop view.
- SYMOR is consistently introduced as “Start your month off right.”
- Museum, Legacy, Community, Visit, Events, and Founder’s Letter each have a dedicated page.
- Lorenzo Washington’s full portrait is shown without cropping.
- Lorenzo’s achievements are highlighted with links to supporting sources.
- Events and Founder’s Letter are structured for a future private dashboard and Google Calendar connection.
- Story sections fade into view; motion is removed when a visitor prefers reduced motion.
- All tour, donation, contact, and social actions route to the museum’s current public pages.

## GitHub Pages

Publish the repository from the `main` branch and root folder in **Settings → Pages**.
