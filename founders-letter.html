A note from Lorenzo.

Monthly news, recent happenings and photographs from the Jefferson Street Sound Museum.

Volume 01 · July 2026

Our history, our sound and our culture remain visible and thriving.

July is a time to celebrate independence, reflect on the freedoms still being protected and remain aware of the conditions affecting our community, friends and loved ones.

SYMOR—Start your month off right—returned with an open mic and artist showcase, welcoming singers, musicians, comedians, spoken-word artists and entertainers to share their gifts.

Visitors can enjoy the museum’s special handkerchief exhibit through the end of August, including examples more than a century old and stories about the history and many uses of these everyday objects.

We also remember trailblazing publisher and civil-rights pioneer Rosetta Miller-Perry. Her work as an advocate, journalist and media owner remains a testament to a life devoted to equal rights and change.

Jefferson Street Sound Museum is honored to be a site on the U.S. Civil Rights Trail. Come step back in time and learn about the artists who performed on Historic Jefferson Street—from Jimi Hendrix, Etta James and Little Richard to Jackie Shane, Ike & Tina Turner and many more.

Many blessings,

Lorenzo Washington

Founder & Curator, JSSM
