A note from Lorenzo.

Monthly news, recent happenings and photographs from the Jefferson Street Sound Museum.

Volume 01 · August 2026

Our history, our sound and our culture remain visible and thriving.

This month at Jefferson Street Sound has been about participation—showing up for our community, making room for artists and continuing to share the stories held inside this museum.

August began with SYMOR—Start Your Month Off Right—bringing singers, musicians, comedians, spoken-word artists and entertainers together for an open mic and artist showcase.

Visitors can also see the museum’s special handkerchief exhibit through the end of August, including examples more than a century old and stories about how these everyday objects carried meaning across generations.

We are proud that Jefferson Street Sound Museum is now part of the U.S. Civil Rights Trail. The music and the movement were never separate here, and this recognition helps more people encounter that complete story.

Come step back in time with us. Hear about Jimi Hendrix, Etta James, James Brown, Little Richard, Peggy Gaines, Jimmy Church, Frank Howard, Jackie Shane, Ike & Tina Turner and so many others connected to Historic Jefferson Street.

Many blessings,

Lorenzo Washington

Founder & Curator, JSSM
