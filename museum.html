Side 01 · The Museum

History, told by the people who lived it.

An intimate collection of artifacts, photographs, sound and firsthand stories from Nashville’s Original Music Row.

Keeper of the sound

Meet Lorenzo Washington.

Lorenzo Washington founded the Jefferson Street Sound Museum in 2011 to preserve the music, people and places that shaped North Nashville. His collection connects national legends with the neighborhood musicians, business owners and audiences who made the street sing.

The museum is deliberately personal. Visitors encounter history through objects and stories gathered by someone who remembers the clubs, the artists and the community around them.

A living archive

The museum’s growing tribute documents the jazz, blues, soul and rhythm & blues heard along Jefferson Street from the 1940s through the 1970s. It also connects that legacy to today through education, creative programming and cultural preservation.
